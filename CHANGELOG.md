# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — Preview (2026-08-15)

First release.

- Dock with **All / Selected → Tri-planar** and **→ UV** buttons.
- Category checkboxes: **Fill layers**, **Mask fills** (texture-driven masks /
  fill effects in mask stacks), and **Generators** (toggles their own
  `Use_Triplanar`-style switch).
- Recurses into groups and into content / mask effects.
- Per-category counts in the status line.
- Requires Substance 3D Painter 10.0+ (layer-stack scripting API).
